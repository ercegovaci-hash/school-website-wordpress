# Volunteer project: website for a public high school (WordPress)

**Live site: [etssd.edu.rs](https://etssd.edu.rs/)**, Economics and Trade High School *(Ekonomsko-trgovinska škola)*, Smederevo, Serbia

Together with fellow students from the Faculty of Organizational Sciences (University of Belgrade), I volunteered to build a new website for a public high school in Smederevo. The goal was to help the school: students, parents and staff needed one place for news, enrollment information, schedules and official documents.

It took a lot of work and attention to detail, and we were proud of the final product. The site is live and in use.

## What the site gives the school

| Audience | What they find |
|---|---|
| **Future students and parents** | School history, educational profiles, enrollment, final and graduation exams, grading criteria, and preparation material for the entrance test |
| **Current students** | Activity calendar: class timetable, supplementary classes, consultation hours, written-test schedule, bell schedule. Also a link to the electronic gradebook (*eDnevnik*) and a student blog with a podcast and journalism section. |
| **Part-time (external) students** | Price list, exam procedure, exam questions, preparatory classes and exam boards |
| **Public and authorities** | Regulations, financial reports, public procurement, the school information guide, and contacts including the data-protection officer |
| **Partners** | International projects (Erasmus+ and others) |

## Technology

- **WordPress** CMS with the **Elementor** page builder
- Content in **Serbian Cyrillic**, structured with menus and submenus for a large number of pages and documents

## What this project shows

- **A real client with real users.** The site had to work for school staff, students and parents, not for a grade.
- **Teamwork** in a group of students, from planning to the finished product.
- **Volunteering.** We used what we learned at university to help a public school.
